# Gemma 3 12B against llama.cpp (RTX 5090)

Model: `bartowski/google_gemma-3-12b-it-GGUF`, `Q4_K_M` (6.79 GiB).
llama.cpp: `third_party/llama.cpp/build-cuda` (`97e4ca7`), same session.

## What was added

The `gemma3` architecture (`Experimental`), following llama.cpp's
`src/models/gemma3.cpp`:

- token embeddings scaled by sqrt(hidden);
- sandwich norms (`post_attention_norm` on the attention output, `post_ffw_norm`
  on the FFN output, both before the residual add); the GGUF norm weights
  already carry Gemma's +1;
- GeGLU with tanh GELU (`ops::geglu_tanh`, ggml's `ggml_gelu` formula);
- sliding-window local layers: `layer % 6 < 5` attend over the last 1024
  positions (`LLAMA_SWA_TYPE_STANDARD`), rotated with base 10000; global
  layers use base 1e6 with linear scaling 8 (frequencies / 8).

Paths: decode runs the per-layer path (GPU matmuls, `attn_block` while the
window covers the context, CPU attention past it). Prefill runs the batched
per-layer path with the fused `prefill_attn_block` while the window covers
the chunk. The whole-token decode, multi-session batching and the fully fused
prefill decline (`token_path_decline`: GeGLU and sandwich norms are not in
those kernels).

## Parity: `allpaka verify --decode 32 --tol 0.3` against `llama-server -ngl 99`

| Prompt | Tokens | Prefill max diff | Decode max diff | Argmax |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `The capital of France is Paris. The capital of Japan is` | 13 | 0.1297 | 0.1666 | 31/32, one near-tie split within tolerance |
| `Write a short poem about the sea.` | 9 | 0.0703 | 0.4045 | 32/32 |
| `def fibonacci(n):` | 7 | 0.1498 | 0.2053 | 32/32 |
| 260 numbered facts + question (crosses the 1024 window) | 3011 | 0.1329 | 0.5668 at step 8 (FAIL at 0.48) | 9/9 before the stop |

The long prompt checks the sliding window: its prefill agrees with llama.cpp
to 0.13. Its decode tail drifts past the 0.48 decode tolerance at step 8 with
argmax still equal, the same Q4 tail noise as on Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B
(`2026-11-14-verify-llama31-8b-rtx5090.md`).

These runs used the per-token prefill. The batched prefill (with the fused
attention block) and the CPU-only path (`ALLPAKA_NO_GPU=1`) give the same
prefill argmax (106) and the same 32 greedy decode tokens
(`tokens-fnv=855bb104785d8b0b`) on `bench --engine`'s prompt.
