Verdicts.

* `ALLPAKA_RFUSE=1` costs about a sixth of decode on qwen3-30b: median -18.07 %, every one
  of 11 admitted pairs below 1 against 3/12 for the null, and the 24-dispatch-per-token
  saving it buys is worth nothing on its own (next bullet). The shipped default - RFUSE off
  - is now measured rather than assumed, so the knob stays off and this is a closed
  negative result with its DoD. The 2026-09-12 prior of 20-27 % was the right sign and a
  little too large.
* `ALLPAKA_RTOPK=1` costs the same -18.04 % for the same -24 dispatches/token, 7/7 below 1.
  The 2026-09-12 prior of ~30 % overstated it by about 12 pp; the sign was right. Stays off.
* `ALLPAKA_RMT=1` folds the same 7680 dispatches and is indistinguishable from the null:
  median +0.82 % with 2/7 below 1, where the pooled null itself put 3/12 below 1 and sat
  1.53 pp off centre. That is not a win - it is the absence of a measurable price at this
  resolution, and a 17.56 pp band means the level is only bounded to roughly the median
  plus or minus half of it. `preregistration.txt` already names the only instrument that
  could price it: TG=1280 on a quiet machine.

The pair that matters most for the rest of the programme is `rmt` against `rfuse`. Two
knobs, the same -7680 dispatches over TG=320, the same token stream, and a measured
difference of 18.9 pp. Dispatch count is therefore NOT the binding constraint on this
model at this TG: removing 24 dispatches a token is free by itself, and RFUSE/RTOPK's cost
is the work the fused kernel adds, not the saving it makes. Consequences recorded where
they bite: `docs/decode-opts.md` RFUSE/RMT/RTOPK/DCOMB rows, and task #37 (extend the
down+combine fold to all 48 layers) loses its mechanism argument - a fold that only removes
dispatches should be expected to be flat, so it needs a per-dispatch-cost story before any
pair is spent on it.


3. NORMFLAG itself
------------------

`null` vs `norm` and `off` vs `norm` are nulls by structure (five identical observables) and
remain nulls by measurement, with the pooled median 1.53 pp off centre inside a 17.56 pp
band. NORMFLAG is not a decode lever on this model: what it decides - the spin-flag
barrier scheme against barriers - moves nothing resolvable at TG=320, and its only
measurable consequence is the reachability of the family above, which runs the other way
(from the per-layer conjunction, not from the bit). The 2026-09-12 era-audit row that read
"110 vs 116 tok/s" for NORMFLAG is therefore about arms that differed by more than the
probe here could see, or about that day's instrument; it is not reproduced and not needed,
because no default depends on it.


4. What this window does not settle
-----------------------------------

* Nothing here is a GLM, 235B or 35B statement. `layer-types.py` says what the family can
  reach on each (GLM 22 of 47 layers, so the prediction there is -7040 dispatches; 235B 4
  of 94, i.e. the family is nearly dead there; 35B 40 of 40 because those layers are GDN and
  have no q/k/v at all), and none of that has been priced with pairs.
* `off_rf` / `off_rmt` / `off_k` (the knobs under NORMFLAG=0, -15360 dispatches) are not in
  this window at all; `drive.py --series` takes them.
* `rmt` stays unpriced at the 3 % level; see section 2.
* Three load vetoes and 14 drift vetoes are recorded in `preregistration.txt`'s amendment.
  The drift rule was wrong and is fixed in `drive.py` for future series; the salvage here is
  arithmetic on rows this run already wrote, at no GPU cost. `analyze.py --selfcheck` pins
  the fixed behaviour, including that a pair where BOTH arms fall together is still vetoed
  and that a load veto is respected whatever the rates say.
