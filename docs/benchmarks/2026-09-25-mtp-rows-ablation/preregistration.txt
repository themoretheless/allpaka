# Pre-registration: price ROWS against the per-row fallback on the only MTP model here

Task #27. Roadmap A9, open lever "MTP on MoE: `q2_k`/`q3_k` have no ROWS
mapping", DoD step (a). Written 2026-09-25T01:28Z (05:28 local), before the
series and after the one pilot pair declared below. Timestamps in this
directory are UTC unless they say `local`.

## The question

`encode_verify_tokens` (crates/allpaka-backend/src/gpu/metal.rs:12602) gives a
speculative verify round one dispatch per expert matrix for all `m` rows when
the routed kernel carries the ROWS mapping, and falls back to `m` indexed
dispatches per matrix when it does not. The fallback fires on `q2_k`/`q3_k`
experts, so the 235B never gets ROWS, and that is the reason its MTP return
condition ("verify is cheaper") never posts. The open step is to price the
difference on a model where BOTH forms can run, because if merging `m` dispatches
into one is not worth a measurable amount there, writing the ROWS branch for
`q3_k`/`q2_k` is not worth writing.

Said as a number: this measures the cost of `3m` indexed dispatches per MoE
layer versus `3`, at equal bytes, equal grid occupancy per row and an identical
emitted stream.

## Arms

One binary, two settings of a kill-switch added for this ablation
(`ALLPAKA_NO_EXPERT_ROWS`, metal.rs:12685 — it only bans the ROWS pipelines,
touching nothing else):

* `rows`   — default. ROWS engages; `ALLPAKA_ROWS_DEBUG` prints `true`.
* `norows` — the per-row indexed fallback carries the whole round.

Model: `models/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-MTP-UD-Q4_K_M.gguf` (40 layers, qwen35moe,
21.1 GiB), `ALLPAKA_DRAFT_K=4` so a round verifies `m = 5` rows. This is the
only local model with an `nextn_predict_layers` block, so it is the only one
where the verify encoder runs.

## What had already been read before this file existed

One pilot pair at 01:26Z, PP=480/TG=32, both arms, same binary:
`rows` 55.8 tok/s speculative against `norows` 56.9 (+2.0% for the fallback),
plain control 95.1 against 95.8, acceptance 21/48 in both, stream PASS in both.
That pair is a pilot and is not evidence: it is one draw of a 2% effect from a
machine whose byte meters sit at 0.75x of published
(`../2026-09-25-q35a3b-postflip/stationarity-vs-level.txt`). The pilot did,
however, reveal the census bug that criteria 3 and 4 below now gate on: the
verify stage's `dispatched` tally was a fixed `4 + 3m` per MoE layer regardless
of which branch ran, so the two arms printed identical dispatch counts while
issuing 480 apart. The tally is now conditional; the fix is part of this change.

## Criteria, fixed before the series

1. Both arms run the same pinned binary copy (sha256 and `git rev-parse HEAD`
   recorded once per run; the tree is dirty by design and the diff is not).
2. Both logs must end with `mtp stream matches plain greedy: PASS`, and the two
   arms must report the same `acceptance a/d`. The knob is allowed to change
   only how the round is dispatched; if the stream or the acceptance moves, the
   pair is void, not slow.
3. The per-round verify dispatch census from `ALLPAKA_MTP_DEBUG` must differ
   between the arms by a positive multiple of `3 (m-1)` per MoE layer, with
   `norows` larger. A pair whose census does not differ did not run the arms it
   claims to have run and is void.
4. `mtp-plain` median, which the kill-switch cannot reach, must agree between
   the arms of a pair to within 2%. Beyond that the window moved during the
   pair; the pair is void.
5. Metric per pair: `mtp-speculative` median of `norows` over that of `rows`,
   read from the report JSON at full float (`measurements[].summary.median`),
   never from stdout. Series statistic: median of the per-pair ratios, the mean
   percent, its SEM, and the sign split by AB/BA order. Orders alternate and
   both must agree in sign for a claim.
6. Threshold: the roadmap step (a) says write the `q3_k`/`q2_k` ROWS branch if
   the ablation shows more than ~3%. So: median ratio >= +3% for `rows` (i.e.
   norows/rows <= 0.97) with consistent sign -> the branch is worth writing;
   |median| < 3% -> ROWS is e2E-neutral on the model that already has it, the
   branch is dropped and the result published as negative; median ratio > +3%
   for `norows` -> the existing ROWS mapping is itself a loss, which opens a
   separate task to retire it from the three kernels that carry it.
7. Window: the machine is not quiet, so no absolute rate is claimed here and no
   comparison to llama.cpp. Everything is a within-run ratio between two arms
   of the same binary, which is the one thing the 14-probe stationarity record
   says survives a depressed window.
8. 20 s between processes. Paired AB/BA. Stop rule: fewer than 8 of the first
   14 pairs admitted by criteria 2-4 -> abandon the series and publish the
   admitted ones with the count stated.

## Cost and DoD

One process is ~55 s (25 s of weight prewarm, ~30 s of the bench itself), so a
14-pair series is about 30 minutes.

DoD: `results.txt` in this directory with the per-pair table, the series
statistics, the decision under criterion 6 written out in the tense of a
result, the roadmap line for the lever updated to point here, and `ALLPAKA_NO_EXPERT_ROWS`
recorded in `docs/decode-opts.md` as an ablation switch rather than a lever.
Reproduce with `python3 drive.py --pairs 14` and `python3 analyze.py`.

## Amendment, 2026-09-25T01:41Z: criterion 4 is a broken meter, and the primary
## statistic moves to the verify stage

Written after the first four pairs of `series1` (started 01:32Z), which are in
`pairs.jsonl` and were visible when this was written. Nothing here rescues the
hypothesis; the four pairs so far all favour the fallback. It fixes what the
series can and cannot decide.

**What broke.** Criterion 4 expected `mtp-plain` to act as a window meter and
agrees within 2%. Observed within-pair plain disagreement as `norows` over
`rows`: -5.4%, +0.4%, -4.3%, -2.0% - i.e. it voids half the pairs. The cause is
not the window. The MTP branch of `crates/allpaka-cli/src/bench.rs` opens both of its sessions as
`new_session_mtp(spec_prompt.len() + 64 + k + 1)`
(crates/allpaka-cli/src/bench.rs:639 for the plain control, :682 for the
speculative run), so the whole speculative section is capped at ~69 decoded
tokens and the driver takes TG=32. That makes the plain control one 0.34 s pass
of 32 tokens and the speculative arm 12 rounds in 0.59 s, inside a process
whose other 50 s are prewarm and the ordinary prefill/decode measurements. A
0.34 s sample cannot resolve a 2% window question on a machine at load 7, and
criterion 4 as written was measuring its own quantisation.

**Consequence for the design.** The e2E question criterion 6 asks ("is ROWS
worth >3% of the token rate") is not decidable at this pass length, because the
lever only touches 12 verify rounds out of a 0.59 s section that also pays 4
draft steps, an `arm_slots` rollback and the round's non-MoE work per token. The
verify stage question ("what does the mapping cost the round it is used in")
IS decidable, and it is the question step (a) was a proxy for: the per-round
`verify N tok: X ms` line from `ALLPAKA_MTP_DEBUG` times exactly the code the
kill-switch changes, is sampled 12 times per process, and takes its median
within the process, so prewarm, capacity ceiling and the 50 s of unrelated work
are all outside it.

**Revised criteria.**

4'. Criterion 4 stays computed and reported as preregistered, and no longer
    voids a pair. It is reclassified as a diagnostic of how much the machine
    moved between the two processes of a pair.
5'. Primary series statistic: median over pairs of `norows verify ms / rows
    verify ms`, with mean percent, SEM and the AB/BA sign split. Secondary: the
    criterion-5 e2E rate ratio, over the pairs criterion 4 would have admitted
    and over all pairs, both reported.
6'. The decision needs the two statistics to agree in sign. If the verify ratio
    says the mapping costs >=3% (ratio > 1.03, consistent sign) the ROWS branch
    is a loss where it exists, `q3_k`/`q2_k` do not get one, and retiring ROWS
    from `matvec_q4_k_mv`, `matvec_q5_k_mv`/`q5_k`, `matvec_q6_k` and
    `matvec_q5_0_mv`/`q8_0_mv` opens as its own task. If they point at a >=3%
    win for ROWS and the e2E statistic is admissible, step (b) opens. If the
    e2E statistic contradicts the verify statistic, publish both and call the
    lever undecided.
7'. The transfer limit, stated rather than argued: this prices the mapping on
    kernels whose experts are q4_k/q5_k/q6_k. The 235B's experts are
    q2_k/q3_k, whose unpack chain is 1.86-2.01x the q4_k one, so per-block
    traffic saved by merging rows costs relatively less there and ROWS could in
    principle pay where it does not here. No local model can test that: the
    235B has no `nextn` block, so `encode_verify_tokens` never runs on it, and
    the only nextn model here quantises its experts above q3_k. The escalation
    path if step (b) ever opens is a q3_k re-quantisation of the 35B-MTP
    experts, not a new kernel written blind.
8'. Stop rule unchanged in kind: abandon if fewer than 8 of the first 14 pairs
    pass criteria 2 and 3 (stream, acceptance, census), which are the checks
    that a pair ran the arms it claims.
