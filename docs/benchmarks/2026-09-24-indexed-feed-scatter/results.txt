# Indexed-rate regime results: feed depth, expert scatter, per-slot activations

Run 2026-09-24/25 against `preregistration.txt` (criteria A, B, C, D for axes 1-2;
E, F for axis 3, added before the axis-3 run). Two processes, both
`cargo test --release -p allpaka-backend --test gpu_ffnbench --
indexed_matvecs_report_effective_bandwidth --nocapture`, Apple M4 Max, 15 shapes,
slots=8, 4 passes, axes interleaved inside the shape loop.

Raw logs: `rates.txt` (feeds 1,8,64 x scatters 0,1) and `rates-xslot.txt`
(feeds 1,64 x scatters 0,1 x x-shared/x-per-slot).

NOTE on `rates.txt`: lines of the form `label [no,ni] sets=N: NN.N GB/s best, ...`
are a legacy print block that had become mis-indexed after the arm loop was
introduced - each of those lines repeats a DIFFERENT shape's numbers. The block
has since been deleted from the harness. Only the `| feed=` arm lines in both
files are valid, and they are what every number below comes from.

## 3. Criteria verdicts

  A (feed lifts q3_k down >= 1.6x) - FAILS, at 1.026x. The published q3_k down
  denominator is not a per_wait=1 regime artefact.
  B (scatter cuts q3_k >= 1.6x but q2_k <= 1.2x) - FAILS, at 1.003x against
  1.009x. Consecutive-slot addressing is not what makes the in-situ down slow.
  C (scatter cuts both formats within +-15%) - FIRES. Every scatter ratio in the
  grid is between 1.001 and 1.020, and scatter is routinely slightly FASTER than
  contiguous (q2_k control, q4_k, q4_k glm). Address layout costs the indexed
  matvec nothing measurable on this machine, so the deficit is not an addressing
  effect and stays a kernel claim.
  D (every feed x scatter ratio within +-15%) - FIRES for the two 235B rows
  (1.026 and 1.058). It does NOT hold across the whole grid, and the exception is
  informative: feed gains of 1.13-1.27x appear on all four q4_k rows (1.129,
  1.257, 1.151, 1.265) and at most 1.064x on any other format. That is not the
  rows-per-dispatch boundary the repo documents (`metal.rs:79-90`, "32k+-row grids
  hide the stall by themselves"): q4_k's 32 768-row row gains 1.129x while q2_k's
  12 288-row row gains 1.058x. It tracks absolute speed instead - q4_k is the
  fastest format in the table (459-533 GB/s at feed=1), so a fixed per-round cost
  is the largest fraction of its window, and feeding removes that fraction.
  Consequence: the feed axis measures the harness's per-round fixed cost, not the
  kernel, and at 221 GB/s the q3_k down shape is far enough from the ceiling that
  the effect is 2.6%.
  E (q3_k loses >= 1.5x from x per slot while q2_k loses <= 1.1x) - FAILS: 0.985x
  against 0.997x.
  F (both formats lose within +-15%) - FIRES. Shared-versus-per-slot activations
  are not the asymmetry either.

## 4. What this closes, and what it does not

Closed: the ledger's per-token time table needs no regime correction. Rebuilding
it on fed rates (the consequence criterion A would have forced) is not warranted,
and no denominator in `time-ledger.txt` moves by more than the noise already
attached to it. The scatter and activation-layout levers are dead as levers:
there is nothing in the indexed harness's addressing or x staging that costs the
q3_k down real time.

Not closed: all three axes were run against the bench's own pipeline, and reading
the model's pipeline construction afterwards shows the bench and the model do not
time the same kernel for a fused-capable down projection. The bench compiles the
indexed down with `swiglu = false` (`indexed_matvec_bandwidth_fed`,
metal.rs:126-128); the model compiles it with `swiglu = true` for every kernel in
`sw_capable` (metal.rs:10752-10798), and `matvec_q3_k_mv` is in that list, so the
q3_k down dispatches in the 235B decode read raw gate AND raw up and apply
silu(gate)*up per activation element. That is the one difference in the pair that
was never measured, and it predicts exactly the asymmetry RUN 2 found (gate_up,
which is never fused, priced at 0.99x; down at 2.09x). It also settles the
dispatch-geometry question that was the last open harness candidate:
`id_grid_kernel` (metal.rs:9473-9475) covers q4_k/q5_0/q8_0 only, so bench and
model both send `matvec_q3_k_mv` through `dispatch_mv(n_out * slots)` at
`mv_tg()` = 128 - the grids are identical and geometry is excluded without a run.

The fused-variant test is registered separately, before its data, in
`../2026-09-24-235b-swfuse/preregistration.txt` (criteria G, H, I).
