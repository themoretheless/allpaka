## Verdict

**Measured, and the default is right: keep `ALLPAKA_DCOMB` unset.** Turning the fold off
costs **+0.868 ms/token** - the median paired delta over the 12 admissible pairs - which
is **11.5 % of the folded per-token time** (7.549 ms/token) on this model. That is about
three times the `~0.3 ms/token` figure the `dcomb()` doc comment in
`crates/allpaka-backend/src/gpu/metal.rs` had been carrying without an A/B behind it.
The registry row in `docs/decode-opts.md` now quotes the measured number, and the comment
quotes this file.

* Primary statistic (the pre-registered one, a ratio of paired rates): median
  `unfold/plain` decode tok/s over 12 admissible pairs **0.896730** = `-10.33 %`,
  SEM 1.23 pp, range 0.8036...0.9856, **12/12 pairs below parity**.
* Level, in the unit the roadmap asks for. Three reductions of the same 12 pairs, stated
  separately so the mixing is visible: paired median **+0.868** ms/token, paired mean
  **+0.933**, range +0.111...+1.688; medians-of-each-arm (132.47 against 117.75 tok/s,
  i.e. 7.549 against 8.493 ms/token) give **+0.944**, which is +12.5 % of the folded
  per-token time and -11.11 % of the unfolded rate. The two bracket each other; the
  paired median is the one to quote, because it is the only one that never compares an
  arm against a level from a different pair.
* Dropped pairs and why, as pre-registered: 2 of 14 real pairs by the load veto
  (pair 4 at max load 11.03, pair 14 at max load 8.67, ceiling 8.0); zero by the drift
  veto, zero by the cold veto (every arm's census 2.63-2.72 s against a 2x-median threshold),
  zero for differing greedy continuations. Criterion 1 held in all 40 arms:
  `declines=0`, `waits=320` (= TG) per arm, `windows=1`, `residency set=true`.
* Structural, and not subject to the noise floor: the `unfold` arm dispatches **+7680**
  kernels per pair (+7680 in all 14 real pairs, i.e. +24 per token at TG=320) while the
  control series moves +0 in all 6 - exactly the combine dispatch and its barrier that the
  fold removes, per token, per layer group. The mechanism is therefore known
  independently of any wall number. (The net is 24/token, not 48: in the layers whose
  down kernel can carry swiglu the dispatch the fold saves is paid back by the standalone
  swiglu it re-introduces. See Corrections at the foot of this file.)
* What this run cannot say: the *level* to the 3 % the pre-registration asked for. The
  control series (identical code, `=1` against unset) admitted all 6 pairs and still spread
  0.8856...1.0323, a band of **8.28 %**, because at ~132 tok/s an arm is a 2.4 s
  sample of the byte pipe and the machine's level moves between two such samples.
  Criterion 2 therefore fails as written, and the pre-registered fallback applies:
  this file reports an interval, not a certified 3 %-precision level. Taking the paired
  SEM (1.23 pp) at face value despite that band, the interval on the decode-rate ratio is
  `0.873...0.921`, i.e. the fold is worth between 7.9 % and 12.7 % of decode tok/s -
  enough to keep the default, not enough to certify a level to a fraction of a ms/token.
* The sign does not need the band. Of all 18 recorded pairs, 14 landed below 1;
  conditioning on that count so no level bias can help either arm, the chance that the
  12 real pairs are exactly the 12 most-downward ones is
  C(14,12)/C(18,12) = 4.9e-03. The control put only 2/6 below 1.

## Mechanism, and why the effect is bigger than the comment claimed

`dcomb()` folds the routed experts' decode `down` into the `combine` dispatch (one
simdgroup per routed slot, the weighted sum reduced through threadgroup memory straight
into `delta`), so a token stops paying a separate combine dispatch, its barrier, and the
per-slot down buffer. At `n_used=8` that is a fixed number of dispatches per token per
MoE layer group, which is what the +24/token census above counts; the wall cost of
removing them is 0.94 ms/token here, not 0.3. Two reasons the earlier figure was low,
both visible in this data: it was a comment, not a measurement, and it was written for a
model class where the fold does not even build - `shared.is_none()` gates it, so GLM and
the 235B never take this path and cannot calibrate it. Qwen3-30B-A3B is the local model
that does, and it has 48 layers' worth of these dispatches per token. (Only 24 of them
come out as a net saving; see Corrections.)

Decode on this machine is byte-pipe limited, so a dispatch-count cut normally buys
nothing - that is the finding of `../2026-09-25-glm-shared-stagger/results.txt`, where
+24 dispatches/token of *pure re-ordering* cost 1.1 %. The difference is that the fold
removes work rather than re-ordering it: `outside-GPU` stays 0-1 % in both arms, so this
is not a scheduling win recovered from idle gaps, it is 320 extra host round trips per
token that no longer happen. (This sentence is wrong as written - both arms report
`waits=320`, so no host round trip is removed; Corrections restates what the fold takes
out instead.)

## Regression check, and one gate condition that is dead

The fold ships by default, so what needs protecting is not the measurement but the
*gate*: if `shared.is_none()` or the even-`hidden` requirement were ever dropped, the
fold would start firing on models whose shapes it was never written for, and the next
wall number on GLM or the 235B would be silently a different code path. So the shape
half of the gate now lives in its own function, `dcomb_shape()` in
`crates/allpaka-backend/src/gpu/metal.rs`, with a CPU-only unit test
(`gpu::metal::tests::dcomb_shape_gate_matches_the_measured_fold`) - CPU-only because the
conditions are shape arithmetic, and because a check that needed a GPU and an 18.6 GiB
model would not run in CI. Mutation-verified, as the roadmap now requires of every check:
dropping the shared gate, dropping the even-hidden test, widening the slot ceiling to 64,
and inverting the two ceilings below each other are each caught by a failing assert; the
restored source passes (`46 passed; 0 failed` for the whole backend lib at
2026-09-25T23:46Z).

What writing the test surfaced: the gate's `n_used <= 32` is dead on every reachable
model. The fused token path itself declines a MoE layer above 16 routed experts
(`router shape` in `decode_token`), so `n_used` in `17..=32` can never reach the fold -
the 32 is the MSL reduce's own width (one 32-lane simdgroup per slot, shadowed at the
call site by the `max_total_threads_per_threadgroup() >= 32 * n_used` pipeline filter),
and it is dominated by a ceiling 16. Both bounds are now named constants
(`DCOMB_MAX_N_USED`, `MOE_DECODE_MAX_N_USED`) and the test asserts they stay in that
order, so if the router-shape decline is ever raised the dead half of this gate becomes
live and says so instead of silently widening the fold.

This section is source-only and changes no behaviour: `dcomb_shape(shared.is_some(),
hidden, *n_used)` is the conjunction that was inline at the call site, and
`MOE_DECODE_MAX_N_USED` is the literal 16 that was in the decline. The measured arms
above were a binary at git `8a16214`, before this refactor; nothing about the fold's
runtime predicate moved, so no re-measure is implied.

## Limits of transfer

Qwen3-30B-A3B at PP=3072/TG=320 only. The gate needs a `q4_k`/`q6_k` matvec `down`
kernel, `shared.is_none()`, even `hidden` and `n_used <= 32`, so GLM-4.5-Air and the 235B
never reach it; the 35B's shared expert and `ALLPAKA_SWFUSE` are different folds with
their own artifacts. Do not quote +0.868 ms/token at another shape.

## What would tighten it (DoD for the level)

The knob needs nothing: ON ships, is 12/12 in sign, and its mechanism is counted exactly.
To price the level to better than the 8 % this instrument resolves, the same driver at
`TG=1280` makes an arm a 9.7 s sample instead of 2.4 s; pre-registering that the control
must first come in under 3 % before any level number is published keeps criterion 2 doing
its job. A second machine state (no live desktop session) would also help and is not
available on demand.

## Corrections, 2026-09-26T00:23Z - why the census is +24/token and not +48

Found the next morning while closing the neighbouring `ALLPAKA_NORMFLAG` series, from a
GGUF header read and a grep, so no GPU and no new numbers: this file's wall result is
unchanged, its two mechanism sentences are not.

**(1) "+24 per token" is 24 layers x 1 dispatch, and the model is type-split.** In
`models/qwen3-30b-a3b-Q4_K_M.gguf`, `attn_v` and `ffn_down_exps` are Q6_K in layers
0,1,2,3,4,5,8,11,14,17,20,23,26,29,32,35,38,41,42,43,44,45,46,47 and Q4_K in the other 24
(llama.cpp's own Q4_K_M rule; the engine prints it in every log as
`Q4K: tensors=289 / Q6K: tensors=49`). `sw_capable` in `metal.rs` lists the down kernels
that carry the fused-swiglu variant - `matvec_q2_k`, `q3_k_mv`, `q4_k_mv`, `q5_k_mv`,
`q8_0`, `q8_0_mv` - and `matvec_q6_k_mv` is *not* among them, so `SWFUSE` (default ON)
drops the standalone swiglu dispatch only in the 24 Q4_K-down layers. Per MoE layer, with
this model's parameters (no shared expert, `ALLPAKA_CFUSE` and `ALLPAKA_DCOMB_SW` at
their default OFF):

| arm | Q6_K-down layers (24) | Q4_K-down layers (24) |
|---|---|---|
| folded (`DCOMB` default) | swiglu 1 + down_combine 1 = **2** | swiglu 1 + down_combine 1 = **2** |
| unfolded (`DCOMB=0`) | swiglu 1 + down 1 + combine 1 = **3** | down 1 (swiglu on load) + combine 1 = **2** |

So the fold removes one dispatch per token in exactly half the layers - 24/token, 7680
over TG=320, which is what both arms of all 14 real pairs measured - and in the other
half it removes no dispatch at all, because the combine it deletes is paid back by the
standalone swiglu it re-introduces. Reproduce with
`python3 ../2026-09-25-normflag-policy/layer-types.py ../../../models/qwen3-30b-a3b-Q4_K_M.gguf`
(capture: `../2026-09-25-normflag-policy/layer-types-qwen3-30b.txt`).

The 24/24 split is conditional on `ALLPAKA_SWFUSE` being ON, because that is what makes
the Q4_K-down layers' unfolded arm skip its swiglu. Run this same A/B with `SWFUSE=0` and
the prediction changes to 48/token (15360): the unfolded arm then dispatches swiglu +
down + combine in every layer. A census that moves with an unrelated knob is a census of
two knobs, so quote the pair together.

**(2) "320 extra host round trips per token" is false.** Both arms report `waits=320` -
that is criterion 1's own evidence, and one command buffer is still one token. What the
fold takes out is 24 kernel launches *inside* that command buffer plus their `bar_c`
drains, so the 0.87 ms/token is GPU-side serialisation between stages, not CPU/GPU round
trips. The distinction matters: it is the reason this dispatch cut paid while the
pure-re-ordering cut in `../2026-09-25-glm-shared-stagger/results.txt` did not.

**(3) What does not move.** The paired median +0.868 ms/token, the 0.873...0.921 interval,
the sign test, the 8.28 % control band and the "default ON is right" verdict are
measurements of the two arms as they are actually encoded, and (1) makes the arm
difference sharper rather than removing it. The `dcomb()` doc comment and the
`ALLPAKA_DCOMB` row in `docs/decode-opts.md` quote the wall figure, which stands.

**(4) The lever this correction exposes.** Getting the fused down+combine to apply swiglu
once per slot row - staged through threadgroup memory rather than re-evaluated per row
pair, which is what `ALLPAKA_DCOMB_SW=1` does and what it measured at -0.8 ms/token -
would extend the fold's saving to the 24 Q4_K-down layers as well. Ceiling by this file's
own measurement: about another +0.9 ms/token (~11 % decode) on this model. Floor: the
`DCOMB_SW` penalty. Both are above this instrument's noise, so the question is worth a
paired series and not an argument.
