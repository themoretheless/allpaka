# q3_k ALU-removal probe: the deficit is not the unpack chain

> The arithmetic conclusion below stands and is what the port was judged on
> (23% ceiling). The mechanism named in "Read" item 4 - that the residual is the
> ushort-vs-uint load width a 110-byte block forces - was tested the same day by
> [q3-load-width-control.txt](q3-load-width-control.txt) and is wrong: halving
> the weight loads moves the probe 1.401 -> 1.311, i.e. <= ~6% and unresolved.

Instrument: `gpu_ffnbench::q5_shape_ratio_against_q4` at the shape Qwen3-235B's
`q3_k` expert downs actually dispatch - `[4096,1536]`, nb=6, `slots`=8,
`sets`=12 (396 MB, streaming), feed=32. All formats in ONE process, interleaved,
`q4_k` as the in-run meter. Two arms per rep; the only difference between them is
`ALLPAKA_Q3_PROBE=nomath`, which swaps `matvec_q3_k_mv` for `matvec_q3_k_nm` -
the incumbent's geometry, its addresses, its activation loads and its 16 weight
words per block-row, with the dequant arithmetic collapsed to one add per load.
That kernel does not compute a dot product, so it is a ceiling, not a candidate.

`q5_k` and `q6_k` are in every run and are untouched by the knob: they are the
control that separates a real q3_k effect from process-to-process drift.
`q6_k` is also the alignment control - its 210-byte block is only 2-aligned like
q3_k's 110, against q4_k's 144 and q5_k's 176, both 16-aligned.

    rep1 off  q4 832.8 1.000 | q5 1488.6 1.788 | q3 1506.7 1.809 | q6 1224.4 1.470
    rep1 on   q4 780.0 1.000 | q5 1401.3 1.797 | q3 1111.2 1.425 | q6 1082.0 1.387
    rep2 off  q4 858.5 1.000 | q5 1556.3 1.813 | q3 1553.4 1.809 | q6 1238.1 1.442
    rep2 on   q4 820.9 1.000 | q5 1448.1 1.764 | q3 1115.7 1.359 | q6 1131.6 1.378

ps/row/block, then the ratio to the same run's `q4_k`.

## Read

1. **Deleting the arithmetic moves `q3_k` 1.809 -> 1.39, and nothing else.**
   The controls repeat to +-1.5% across all four runs (q5 1.76-1.81, q6
   1.38-1.47), so the -23% on the probed arm is the arithmetic and the
   incumbent's 1.809 matches the 1.75-1.82 published from the quiet curve-fit
   work. Roughly three quarters of `q3_k`'s per-block cost is still there with
   the unpack chain gone.
2. **The residual is not bytes.** Per byte of block: `q4_k` 5.8 ps/B, `q6_k`
   5.8, `q5_k` 8.5, `q3_k` 13.7 - and 10.1 with no arithmetic at all. `q6_k`
   reaches `q4_k`'s byte rate while carrying 46% more bytes per block, so the
   byte pipe is not what `q3_k` falls out of.
3. **Alignment is not it either.** The 2-aligned `q6_k` is the cheapest format
   per byte after `q4_k`; the 16-aligned `q5_k` is 1.5x worse.
4. What is left in the probe is the load *instruction stream*: 16 ushort weight
   reads per block-row against `q4_k`'s ~4 uint reads of the same span, because
   a 110-byte block leaves every second block 2-aligned and MSL forbids a
   4-byte vector load from a 2-aligned address. 2.5x the load instructions,
   ~1.7x the residual cost.

**Consequence.** The port this section was written to justify - `q4_k`'s
per-block extraction structure inside `matvec_q3_k_mv` - is capped at the 23%
the probe recovered, and a real kernel has to keep the arithmetic it deletes.
Not pursued; see [README.md Correction #5](README.md) for the reversal of the
earlier "the fix is the inner loop" claim.

## Caveats on this run

Load average 8-16 throughout (Spotlight at 96%, two chat runtimes at ~99% each,
`xtask` at 99%), and `q4_k`'s absolute reading is 780-859 ps/row/block against
the ~282 the same shape produced on a quiet machine - a 3x contention factor on
the absolute. The ratios are what this result rests on, and they are supported
by (a) two untouched controls repeating across the arms and (b) the incumbent's
ratio agreeing with the quiet-machine value. Do not quote the absolute numbers,
and re-run both arms at load < 5 before any follow-on decision.

## How the first version of this probe was wrong

Worth keeping, because it is the same trap twice in one day. The first probe
consumed only 2 of its 32 activation registers, so the compiler deleted the
other six `float4` loads: the "no arithmetic" arm measured an eighth of the
activation traffic and came out at 0.73x `q4_k` - better than the memory-bound
format, which should have been the tell. After forcing every load to reach the
output the same arm reads 1.39. Load liveness, not source text, is what a probe
kernel has to preserve.

## Repro

```sh
cd "$(git rev-parse --show-toplevel)"
for arm in off on; do
  env ALLPAKA_Q3_PROBE=$([ $arm = on ] && echo nomath) \
      ALLPAKA_Q5AB_N_OUT=4096 ALLPAKA_Q5AB_N_IN=1536 \
      ALLPAKA_Q5AB_SETS=12 ALLPAKA_Q5AB_PER_WAIT=32 \
      cargo test -p allpaka-backend --test gpu_ffnbench -- \
      --ignored --nocapture --test-threads=1 q5_shape_ratio
done
```
