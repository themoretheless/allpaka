Against `preregistration.txt` (criteria G, H, I for Phase S; J, K for the bench
arm). Machine Apple M4 Max, model `qwen3-235b-a22b-instruct-2507-Q2_K_XL.gguf`,
PP 480 / TG 64, `ALLPAKA_PROFILE=max-performance ALLPAKA_BENCH_SKIP_MTP=1`,

Split instrument on (`ALLPAKA_DECODE_SPLIT=1`). Raw runs `s1-base.txt`,
`s1-swf0.txt` ... `s3-swf0.txt` (+ `.json`).

## 4. Mechanism, and why it is not a curiosity

The `SWIGLU_X` variant applies silu(gate)*up at the point where the down kernel
loads its activation (`if (SWIGLU_X) { v = sw4(v, u1v[g]); }`, in the q3_k
indexed loop at metal.rs:1439-1441). A down projection reads its activation once
per OUTPUT ROW: the [4096,1536] x8 dispatch re-loads and re-computes the same
12 288 activation elements 4096 times, so per token the fused path spends 79
dispatches x 32 768 rows x 1536 elements = 4.0 G element-swiglus and 4.0 G x 4 B
= 15.9 GB of activation reads through the cache hierarchy where the unfused
dispatch reads 8.0 GB, to save 79 dispatches and 79 encoder fences. RUN 2
measured encoder fences at 0.0 ms/token over the whole 1318-dispatch token, so
the thing fusion was buying was already free, and the standalone swiglu it
removes costs 0.20 ms/token - it computes each of the 12 288 elements once.

Both halves of the fused path's price scale with `n_out * n_in * slots` rather
than with weight bytes, which is the prediction to keep: 4.0 G element-swiglus
and 8 GB of extra cache traffic for 7.31 ms is ~1.1 TB/s of extra activation
reads plus ~550 G silu/s, both plausible for the machine, and neither is a DRAM
figure. It is why `gate_up` (n_out 1536, and never fused) shows nothing of the
kind: the redundancy is proportional to the number of rows that re-read one
activation vector, and the down projection is the wide-in-that-sense matrix.

## 5. Status of the decision

Phase S attributes the time. It does not ship anything: split mode costs
13-16% of the token, so the default flip needs the wall-only paired A/B (Phase W,
10 pairs, AB/BA alternating, `w*.txt`) and the e2E gate the q5_0 flip used. The
bench-side fused arm (criteria J, K) is the cross-check that the micro-bench can
reproduce the 1.89x on the identical weight bytes. Phase W below settles the
first of these; the bench arm and the cross-model arm are still open.

## 6. Consequences for the roadmap, independent of Phase W

  - The q3_k unpack chain is no longer the explanation of the 235B's decode gap.
    Its in-situ stage price is now 202 GB/s against its own bench rate of 221.3
    GB/s, i.e. the kernel is close to its measured limit; what was slow was the
    fusion policy wrapped around it.
  - `ALLPAKA_SWFUSE` is ON by default for every kernel in `sw_capable`
    (q2_k, q3_k, q4_k, q5_k, q8_0, q5_0 downs) and for the dense FFN as well, so
    this is not a 235B-only knob: every MoE and dense model with a fused down is
    paying a per-row silu recompute. The same stage-level A/B has to be run on
    the 30B and GLM before a global default flip is justified.

## 10. Decision

Ship the global flip: `ALLPAKA_SWFUSE` becomes opt-IN, `sw_capable` unchanged.
Justification: it is +18.0% on the 235B's wall (10/10 signs), +0.8% predicted on
GLM (measured flat inside its own drift band), exactly nothing on the 30B, and
the only format where fusion is free (q8_0, 0.99x) saves 79 dispatches/token
that Phase S measured at 0.04 ms - the thing fusion bought was already free.
A per-format policy would add a table of exceptions to protect a 1% model, so
the default flips for every kernel that can fuse.

## 11. What the knob does not reach

The fold is wired in exactly two places, both in the decode pipeline builders:
`metal.rs:10706` (dense `TokenFfn::Dense`) and `metal.rs:10816` (the MoE expert
group, where `shared_down_sw` additionally refuses fusion unless the shared
expert's down carries the variant too). The batched FFN path - `gpu::ffn_batch`
(`metal.rs:17115`) and `ffn_batch_grouped` (`metal.rs:16258`), reached from
`QuantMat::ffn_many` (`quantmat.rs:271`) and `ffn_grouped` (`quantmat.rs:486`) -
encodes gate, up, a standalone `("swiglu", 1, 1)` pass and a down built with the
three-argument `gpu.pipeline(kernel, rows, lpr)`, which has no swiglu parameter
at all. So prefill never fused, this flip changes nothing there, and the prefill
mm TFLOPS question stays independent of it.

That was not obvious from the knob's name, and the first regression test written
for it was vacuous because of exactly this: a backend-level A/B on `ffn_many`
issued 4 dispatches per item in *both* settings, so it agreed bit-for-bit while
testing nothing and was deleted. The surviving
check lives at the model level, where the fold is live, and asserts the counts
apart from the numbers: `crates/allpaka-model/tests/swfuse_fold_is_scheduling.rs`
measures 2480 dispatches over 8 tokens folded against 2704 standalone on
`qwen3-0.6b-Q8_0`, requires `d_plain > d_folded` to call the pair non-degenerate,
and then requires the argmax chains and the final logits to agree exactly
(max |Δ| = 0.0). A future batched-path fusion therefore needs its own arm here,
not this one.

Two open consequences of the same reading:

  - The batched path has never been priced against `SWIGLU_X` at `m > 1`. The
    recompute factor is per output row of the down (`n_out` loads of the
    activation block) and does not shrink with the batch, so the expectation is
    that fusion loses there too - but that is an extrapolation from `m = 1`
    arithmetic, not a measurement.
  - Every model whose expert or dense down is in `sw_capable` was fused before
    this flip and is not now. That includes Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (`q5_k` down, bench
    price 0.733x), so its published decode row is stale by policy - it should
    read faster, not slower - and needs its own paired re-run before the number
    in `allpaka-vs-llama-metal-status.md` is quoted again.

## 13. Annotation added 2026-09-25 - sections 7 and 12 recomputed at full precision from their own JSONs

The `.json` beside every `.txt` in this directory carries
`.measurements[decode].summary.median` as a float (`28.63956474255374`), while the
`.txt` prints seconds to 2 decimals, which at TG=64 quantises ms/token at 0.156.
Section 7 flagged its own SEM as "partly a print artefact". It is not, and that is now
checked rather than assumed. One refinement to that flag first: the same stdout carries
*two* precisions - `tok/s` printed to 1 decimal is ~16x finer than the seconds field
(+/-0.01 ms/token at these rates against 0.156), and sections 7 and 12 derived their
ms/token from the coarse one (1.33 s / 64 = 20.78) while section 9's percentages come
from the fine one (99.5 / 97.4 = +2.16%, published "+2.2%"). So the coarser read was
chosen, not the only one available. The rule is: the JSON median, or failing that the
tok/s column, never the seconds column. Everything below is a recomputation of the
coarse reads; section 9's percentages survive it unchanged:

| statistic | from the txt (published above) | from the JSON |
|---|---|---|
| pairs 2-10 mean Δ | -7.708 ms/token | **-7.7223** |
| pairs 2-10 SEM | 0.239 | **0.2403** |
| pairs 2-10 signs | 9/9 negative | 9/9 negative |
| pairs 2-10 gain | +18.0% | +18.07% |
| all 10 mean Δ / SEM | -8.594 / 0.911 | -8.6080 / 0.9114 |

So the print cost 0.014 ms in the mean and 0.001 ms in the SEM. The spread the SEM
measures is real pair-to-pair spread, and it is dominated by one pair: the Δ series
is -16.5795, -9.5531, then eight values in -7.22..-7.98. Dropping pair 2 as well as
pair 1 gives the sharpest bound this same data supports - **pairs 3-10: Δ = -7.4935
ms/token, sd 0.2350, SEM 0.0831, 8/8 signs, +17.64%** - which is 90x its own SEM.
The pre-registered exclusion rule was pair 1 only, so -7.708 +/- 0.239 stays the
headline; 0.0831 is offered as what the series looks like once both leading pairs
are set aside, not as a replacement chosen after seeing the deltas.

Two corrections to section 7's own wording, both from the same recompute:

  - "the honest spread of the stable cluster is +/-0.16 ms base, +/-0.0 arm" - the
    second half is a print artefact. Over pairs 3-10 the fold-off arm spans
    34.908-35.139 ms/token (sd 0.078), not zero; the fold-on arm is sd 0.278. The
    clean-looking arm was clean only because the printer rounded it.
  - section 12's clean-pair mean is -7.2105 ms/token, not -7.27 (reps -7.4160 and
    -7.0050). Its agreement with Phase W is therefore 0.51 ms, not 0.44.

One structural fact these artifacts pin, and it is the reason to read the JSON on
any future run: `samples_tok_s` has exactly **one element** in every `w*.json` and
`p*.json` here, and `summary` has p10 == p90 == median. A run reports a single
whole-token-stream rate, so there is no within-run error bar at all in this
instrument - the pair is the only noise estimate available, which is what makes
pair count the knob that buys precision (10 pairs -> SEM 0.08-0.24).

Consequence recorded where it bites: for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at ~9.8 ms/token and TG=32
the txt step is 0.3125 ms/token, i.e. 1.4x the low end of that model's pre-registered
0.23-0.64 ms band. A txt-read pair there would be quantised away before it started.
See [`../2026-09-25-q35a3b-postflip/preregistration.txt`](../2026-09-25-q35a3b-postflip/preregistration.txt).

The rule these 16 JSONs buy, which is now the admission gate on every pair in this repo:
**gate the arms against each other, not against history.** An arm's absolute prefill can
sit inside the published band while the pair still carries half a millisecond of phantom,
because a 3% within-pair prefill drift is worth ~0.2 ms on a 7 ms/token model - the whole
size of GLM's effect. So: admit a pair only if |prefill(A)/prefill(B) - 1| <= 1%, print
that ratio per pair beside the decode delta, and discard rather than correct - applying
px30 rep1's own 1.1836 prefill ratio as a correction turns its -0.54 into +0.66, so the
correction manufactures a phantom of its own. Against that floor the
[`../2026-09-25-q35a3b-postflip/preregistration.txt`](../2026-09-25-q35a3b-postflip/preregistration.txt)
band of 0.23-0.64 ms/token is 6.7-19 null floors, so that run is resolvable on 10 pairs -
but only with the ratio gate on. Taking GLM's 0.086 two-rep spread (sd 0.061) as the
pair-noise proxy for a model where the fold does engage puts the 10-pair SEM near
0.019 ms/token, five times below the band's low end.
