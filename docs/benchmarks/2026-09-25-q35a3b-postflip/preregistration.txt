# Pre-registration: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B decode on the post-flip binary

Task #25. Blocked on a quiet window since 2026-09-24 21:41 (an unrelated debug
binary held two cores at 200% and the indexed meter read 0.25x of published -
see [`../2026-09-25-narrow-shape-axes/results.txt`](../2026-09-25-narrow-shape-axes/results.txt)

## Question and DoD

What does `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M.gguf` decode at on the shipped binary
(`ALLPAKA_SWFUSE` unset = no fold), and what did it decode at under the policy
that was live until 2026-09-24 (`ALLPAKA_SWFUSE=1` = fold the SwiGLU into the
down activation loads)? Verdict: a paired ms/token delta with its SEM and sign
count, plus the resulting change to this model's published llama ratio - or an
explicit "below the instrument" line with the SEM that establishes that.

## Prediction, stated as a band because the shape is unpriced

Folding costs `t_plain x (1/r - 1)`, where `r` is the fused/plain byte-rate
ratio for that format at the dispatched geometry. For `q5_k` routed down:
`t_plain = 203.5 MiB / 345 GB/s = 0.62 ms`.

  - at the measured q5_k ratio 0.733 ([4096,1536],
    [`../2026-09-24-235b-swfuse/results.txt`](../2026-09-24-235b-swfuse/results.txt)
    section 3) the saving is 0.23 ms/token = +2.3% tok/s;
  - the model's real down shape is [2048,512], which has NO measured ratio; the
    measured ratios across formats and geometries span 0.491-0.874, and the
    0.491 end would put the saving at 0.64 ms/token = +6.5%.

Band: +0.23 to +0.64 ms/token, +2.3% to +6.5% decode. Phase W's paired wall
harness resolved 7.7 ms at SEM 0.24 in ten pairs, so a 0.23 ms effect is at the
edge of what that instrument sees and a 0.64 ms effect is comfortably inside it.
The band straddles the noise floor, which is exactly why this is a run to be
done rather than a number to be settled by arithmetic.

One part of that borrow is already bounded by existing data, and it is the
layout part rather than the shape part: every fused rate in the ladder was
measured with the slots SHARING one activation block (`INDEXED_X_PER_SLOT =
false`, correct for gate/up), while a routed down gives each slot its own. For
q3_k at [4096,1536] that difference is ~2% - the x-shared bench fused arm reads
108.9 GB/s against the model's own per-slot fused `down` stage at 106.8, the
0.98 closure in section 3 of the swfuse artifact. What stays unpriced is the
shape: two blocks per row instead of six.

A 2x2 harness for exactly that question exists as of 2026-09-25:
`cargo test --release -p allpaka-backend --test gpu_ffnbench -- q35_down_shape
--ignored --nocapture` with `ALLPAKA_BENCH_FEEDS=1`
(`gpu_ffnbench.rs::q35_down_shape_priced_fused_and_plain_across_x_layouts`).
Run it BEFORE the wall pairs - it is 40 s, it gates on its own meters (x-shared
plain must read within 3% of 468.8 / 344.9), and if it says the per-slot fold
ratio is not 0.733 the band above collapses to a point and the wall run's
expected effect is known in advance instead of chased.
ATTEMPTED 2026-09-25 22:25 UTC, twice; both attempts rejected by that gate, and the
quantity it was built for did not reproduce - per-slot fold ratio 0.845 then 0.556
across two processes of the same harness 90 s apart, with the control shape moving the
same way (0.759 then 0.557). See [`harness-rejected.txt`](harness-rejected.txt). The
band above therefore stays a band: the wall pairs are the only admissible route to it,
and a future pre-flight run needs two agreeing harness processes plus the meter gate,
not one.

Note the direction of the shape risk: the fold's extra ALU scales as
`n_out x n_in` and so do the bytes it sits on, so the ratio is geometry-weak at
fixed format - the [4096,1536] -> [2048,512] extrapolation is the one unverified
step, and it is why the middle of the band is the honest expectation.

## Harness (nothing to build; commands verbatim from the model's bench slot doc)

    ALLPAKA_BENCH_SKIP_MTP=1 ALLPAKA_BENCH_PP=480 ALLPAKA_BENCH_TG=32 \
      target/release/allpaka bench --engine models/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M.gguf

paired as in `scripts/glm-ab-stagger.sh`, arms `unset` vs `ALLPAKA_SWFUSE=1`
(the shipped default takes the fast arm now; `=1` recreates the policy that was
default-ON from 2026-09-10 to 2026-09-24, which is what every published row for
this model measured), AB/BA alternating, >= 10 pairs.

Precision, fixed 2026-09-25 against the 235B's own artifacts (sections 13 and 14 of
[`../2026-09-24-235b-swfuse/results.txt`](../2026-09-24-235b-swfuse/results.txt)):
take ms/token from `.measurements[decode].summary.median` in the JSON written by
`ALLPAKA_BENCH_REPORT` (a float, e.g. `28.63956474255374`). If only stdout survives,
use its `tok/s` column and never its seconds column - that print carries two
precisions, and the seconds field at TG=32 steps at 0.3125 ms/token, 1.4x the low end
of the band this run exists to test, while its tok/s column steps near 0.01. Recomputing
section 7's ten pairs from their JSONs moved the headline by only 0.014 ms (a 7 ms
effect is insensitive to a 0.156 ms step), but the same recompute on the shipped-instrument
cross-model pairs changed a published figure: GLM's fold cost is 0.231 ms/token, not the
-0.31 the print showed twice, and a 30B pair whose arms print identical 6.72 ms/token
actually differs by 0.0344. This model's band starts at 0.23, so it is squarely in the
regime where the read matters.

10 pairs, not 5, because the pair is this instrument's only error bar: every 235B
run reports one whole-stream rate (`samples_tok_s` has 1 element, p10 == p90 == the
median), so there is no within-run spread to quote and precision is bought purely by
pair count - ten AB/BA pairs there gave SEM 0.083-0.240 ms/token on a settled
window. "Below the instrument" is an acceptable verdict only once all ten are in
with their signs reported per pair.

Admission gates, fixed now. (1) Pair-level prefill agreement: admit a pair only if
|prefill(A)/prefill(B) - 1| <= 1%, and print that ratio beside the decode delta. The
30B no-op control is what prices this: its gated rep reads 0.0344 ms/token of phantom
while its ungated sibling, whose arms' prefill differed by 18.4%, reads 0.5403 - more
than twice this model's whole predicted band. Do not drift-correct, discard: applying
that pair's own prefill ratio as a correction flips its -0.54 into +0.66. (2) The
run's own dispatch census must show the two arms differ (the 235B reads 1397 vs 1318 per token, and the GLM pair reproduced its
published census 824 vs 778 exactly). Equal dispatch counts means the fold arm
did not engage on this model's geometry and the pair measures nothing. Second
gate: prefill inside its published band (1412-1449 tok/s); the 235B's rejected
rep 3 was caught by prefill halving while the decode signs inverted.

Report the delta in ms/token against the model's own GPU clock, and only then
convert to tok/s. If both gates pass and |delta| < 1 SEM, publish the neutral
result and leave the 1.35x row labelled as the fused-regime figure it is.

## Amendment 1, fixed before the run (2026-09-25 00:30 UTC)

Two departures from the protocol above, both written down before any pair of the
real series ran, on the evidence of one pilot pair (`harness-pilot.txt`):

1. **A llama arm joins each pair.** The question in the DoD ends at "the
   resulting change to this model's published llama ratio", and that ratio
   (1.35x) was measured in the fused regime on 2026-09-12. Restating it needs a
   llama number from the same window, not arithmetic across two - this repo has
   already withdrawn a 4.8x/3.9x column for exactly that mistake
   ([../qwen35-35b-m4-max-pending.md](../qwen35-35b-m4-max-pending.md), "Why the
   ratio column was withdrawn"). One `llama-bench -p 0 -n 32 -d 1 -r 1` per
   pair, placed between the two allpaka arms so any drift hits both equally.
2. **The absolute prefill band is reported, not applied as a rejection.** Gate 3
   asks prefill to sit in 1412-1449 tok/s. The pilot reads 1246-1251, i.e. 0.87
   of the band, while the two arms agree to 0.40% - a uniform desktop tax on the
   window, which is not what that gate was written for (its stated purpose is
   catching the 235B's rep 3, where prefill *halved* between arms while the
   decode signs inverted). The pair-level 1% gate and the dispatch-census gate
   are what protect a paired delta, and both are applied. A pair that fails
   either is discarded; the band miss is printed per pair so the reader can see
   the window's absolute level rather than have it hidden by a rejection.

The pilot pair, kept as the harness check rather than as data
(`harness-pilot.txt`): plain 10.2227981875 ms/token against fused
10.7707174375 (+0.5479192500), prefill 1245.8704844125095/1250.8834625054667
(ratio 1.004024), dispatch census 24384 vs 23200 for 32 tokens. The census is the proof that the
`=1` arm really recreates the old policy: 23200/32 = 725 per token, the exact
figure the 2026-09-12 warm artifact prints for the then-default-ON build. The
delta direction is the same as the 235B's (folding costs), and its size lands in
the upper half of the 0.23-0.64 ms band predicted from the `q5_k` rate ratio.

## Amendment 3, fixed before the pre-flight (2026-09-25 04:41 local, 00:41 UTC-less-8)

The pre-flight requirement the band section states in prose - two agreeing
harness processes plus the meter gate, not one - is now code:
[`preflight.py`](preflight.py). It runs
`gpu_ffnbench::q35_down_shape_priced_fused_and_plain_across_x_layouts` from the
already-built test binary, invoked directly so that the 16 h-stuck
`cargo test --no-run` belonging to another session cannot put a target-dir lock
between the probe and the window it is trying to catch. It probes when the
1-minute load average is <= 7.0 - deliberately above the 5.0 that tasks #10 and
#15 used as a wall-run ceiling, because this is a 15 s self-rejecting probe rather
than a 50 min run, and because the two rejected attempts of 2026-09-25 happened at
loads 5.49 and 5.55, which prices `load < 5` as insufficient for a ratio anyway.
What decides an attempt is not the load number but gates A-C:

  - (A) both x-shared plain meters land within 3% of published - `q4_k`
    [4096,1536] 468.8 and `q5_k` [4096,1536] 344.9,
  - (B) all sixteen arm spreads are <= 1.03x,
  - (C) two A/B-passing attempts agree on all eight fold/plain ratios (4 shapes
    x 2 activation layouts) within 5%.

Gate C is the one that carries the weight: it is exactly the test the 22:25 UTC
pair would have failed, and A+B alone would have admitted both of them.

Every attempt's full stdout is kept as `attempt-N.txt`, which is the other thing
[`harness-rejected.txt`](harness-rejected.txt) asks for: its own attempt 2
survived only as grep-filtered lines, so nothing beyond three-decimal ratios
could be recovered from a run that had already been rejected. On a C-pass the
script prints the collapsed band - the mean per-slot fold ratio at [2048,512],
the same-format control ratio at [4096,1536] as the window's own temperature, and
`0.62 ms x (1/r - 1)` - and only then hands the window to `run-pairs.sh`.

Sanity check before trusting the gate: pointed at the archived
[`harness-attempt-1.txt`](harness-attempt-1.txt) it reproduces that attempt's
rejection verbatim - meters 0.83x and 0.86x of published, worst spread 1.036x,
down per-slot 0.845 against the control's 0.759. The code and the human read of
the same log agree, so a PASS from here is this protocol's verdict, not a new
instrument's.

If the script times out without a C-pass, that is itself the result to publish:
the band stays 0.23-0.64 ms/token, the shape question stays open, and the wall
pairs remain the only admissible route to either.
