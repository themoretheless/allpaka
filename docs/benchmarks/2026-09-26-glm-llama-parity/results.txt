## Verdict

**The 0.86× row is dead. GLM-4.5-Air decode on the current build is at parity with
llama.cpp, point estimate slightly above 1, and this window cannot certify a win.**

| statistic | value | n |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| median per-pair decode ratio (admitted) | **1.0578×** | 7 |
| median per-pair decode ratio (all counted pairs, no gates) | **1.0446×** | 12 |
| mean of admitted | +3.88% (SEM 3.51) | 7 |
| admitted ratio interval | 0.846 .. 1.150 | — |
| AB / BA halves | 1.0539 / 1.1052 (gap 5.13 pp, **same sign**) | 5 / 2 |
| pairs above parity | 6 of 7 | — |
| cross-pair spread | 30.44 pp | — |

**Certification: the level is NOT certifiable, so this artifact publishes the
interval, not the point.** The pre-registered test was cross-pair spread and the
AB/BA gap against the repo's own published identical-code null floor of 8.28 pp
([2026-09-25-dcomb-ab](../2026-09-25-dcomb-ab/), pessimistic bookend 17.56 pp from
[2026-09-25-normflag-policy](../2026-09-25-normflag-policy/)). The AB/BA gap passes
(5.13 pp < 8.28 pp, same sign); the cross-pair spread fails by 3.7× (30.44 pp).
Under the pre-registered verdict bands the admitted median 1.0578 is mechanically a
**win** (>1.05), but the unfiltered median 1.0446 is inside the 0.95-1.05 **parity**
band, and SEM 3.51 pp on n=7 is wider than the 5 pp distance between them. The
claim allowed by this data is therefore *"parity, point estimate above 1"*, and a
`>1.05` row would be reading the gate ledger rather than the measurement — see the
bias disclosure below.

## What this run does and does not replace

- **Replaces** the decode cell of the GLM row in
  [../allpaka-vs-llama-metal-status.md](../allpaka-vs-llama-metal-status.md) and
  gap 1's arithmetic. It confirms the direction the arithmetic guessed (0.86 → ~1.0)
  **by measurement**. The mechanism underneath it has an independent witness in
  another artifact — the `q5_0_mv` merge's own clean band, **+16.05% decode**, SEM
  1.59, 15/15 pairs positive
  ([../2026-09-24-q5-0-mv-e2e/paired-32.txt](../2026-09-24-q5-0-mv-e2e/paired-32.txt))
  — and *not* in this run's absolutes: that artifact's post-port anchor, 45.95 tok/s,
  was printed at pp480/**tg64** while every number here is tg32, so reading 46.04 and
  45.95 as the same quantity is a cross-geometry comparison. Its paired *delta* is
  what transfers; its absolute is not (its own file says as much). See "The incumbent
  moved too" for the arithmetic done the honest way.
- **Does not replace** the GLM *prefill* cell (`~1.4×`). See "Prefill" below.
- **Does not** speak for any other model, other prompt/generation lengths, other
  builds, or e2E chat behaviour.

### Disclosure: A3 is on allpaka's channel, so its firing biases the ratio up

This is the one thing in this artifact that could be used to over-claim, so it is
stated plainly. The four A3-removed pairs read a median ratio of **1.0003**; the
admitted set reads **1.0578**. The disturbance A3 detects is **allpaka-side** — in
the removed pairs allpaka's prefill median is 277.7 against 357.7 admitted, while
*llama's* prefill in those same pairs is **higher** (413.4 vs 385.3) — so removing
pairs because one arm looks disturbed mechanically lifts a two-arm ratio.

The saving arithmetic, and the reason the honest verdict is still parity-or-better
rather than "uninterpretable": the whole gate ledger moves the headline by
**1.32 pp** (1.0446 unfiltered → 1.0578 admitted), which is 6× *below* the 8.28 pp
identical-code null floor. The gates are not doing the work — but neither is the
margin, because 1.32 pp is also the distance from the win band's edge. Also note the
one-sided band weakness pre-registered as a limitation: A3's floor is a floor with
no ceiling, so a *faster*-than-calibrated window would pass unremarked, and round3
pair1 (ratio 0.8458, ap_pp 322.8) squeaks past A3 by 1.5% while being the pair that
sets the interval's low end.

## The incumbent moved too (invariant 5)

llama's GLM tg32 in this window: **43.43** median admitted, 37.90-44.11 excluding
the A4 outlier. Its own 2026-09-12 cool anchor is **45.46 ± 0.21** at build 10809 —
so llama's arm is **−4.5%** versus that anchor at build 10964 in a warmer window.
The old 0.86× was allpaka's pre-`q5_0_mv` **39.0** against llama's **45.46**, both
printed at pp480/tg32 in the 2026-09-12 cool matrix — so the two arms of that cell
did share a geometry, and the objection to it is that neither arm has been measured
at this build, not that its tg was mismatched.

What is independent of this run is the *mechanism's own paired measurement*: the
`q5_0_mv` clean band, **+16.05% decode** (SEM 1.59, 15/15 pairs positive, trimmed
+15.95%). Applying it gives 0.86 × 1.1605 ≈ **1.00** — parity reachable from
allpaka's arm alone plus llama's *unchanged* anchor, with no help from llama's
−4.5% drift. Two limits on that product, both stated rather than smoothed: the
+16.05% was measured at pp480/**tg64**, so it carries the merge's delta across
generation length (paired-32 itself warns that its absolutes are not comparable to
tg32 reads), and the 0.86 it starts from was a cool-window matrix row, never a pair
at this build. So 1.00 is a consistency check on the direction, and this run's
1.045× is the measurement.

The form to avoid — which is what an earlier draft of this section had — is the
circular one: multiplying *this* run's 46.04 and 43.43 back into the old ratio
reproduces 1.0592 exactly, and proving nothing, because 46.04/43.43 *is* this run.

## Prefill: this run gives no support to the `~1.4×` cell

In this design allpaka's prefill is the A3 thermometer, not a claim, and it was
deliberately paired against a channel the repo already documents as near-unusable
(llama GLM pp480 `255.21 ± 107.51`, ±42%, from
[../2026-09-12-matrix/driver.txt](../2026-09-12-matrix/driver.txt)). Here llama's
pp480 spread 298.6 → 414.1 across 12 pairs (38.7%), reproducing that instrument
verdict. What the run *can* say: on the current build, in this window, allpaka
pp480 median **357.7** against llama **385.3** reads **0.93×** on the admitted
pairs and 0.85× over all 12 — the opposite side of 1.0 from the `~1.4×` cool-matrix
cell, and no pair in this run reaches it. That is a reason to **re-measure the GLM
prefill cell properly (cool, paired, prefill as the measured quantity, 1.4× as the
incumbent to beat)**, not a claim that 1.4× is wrong: this window is not cool and
this cell was not this run's question. The row stays `~1.4× — 2026-09-12 cool
matrix, unconfirmed at pp480 by the 2026-09-26 parity run` until that re-measure.

## What is not claimed

- No claim about any model other than GLM-4.5-Air Q4_K_M, any shape other than
  pp480/tg32, any build other than the pinned `7b8ec67a…` binary, or MTP/e2E/chat
  behaviour (`ALLPAKA_BENCH_SKIP_MTP=1`).
- No claim resting on the 5 removed pairs, and no claim of a **win** — the
  certification test failed on cross-pair spread, so the number is an interval
  (0.846-1.150 admitted; 1.0446-1.0578 depending on whether the gate ledger is
  applied) around a point estimate that straddles the parity/win boundary.
- Not a same-process-identical-token comparison: the engines' token streams are not
  identical, and the third difference named in the pre-registration stands — this
  run pairs llama's `-p 0 -n 32 -d 480` against allpaka's pp480-then-32, which is
  the closest same-command reading available but not byte-identical state.
- The n=2 BA half is thin; it agrees in sign and its median (1.1052) is the higher
  of the two, so the order split does not support a "first arm is faster" artifact
  here — but with 5 AB and 2 BA admitted, order balance is this run's weakest
  design property and any re-run should force equal halves.

## Definition of done / next

Gap 1's stated requirement — a same-command llama arm on the current build — is met,
so the gap closes as **parity (interval, not certifiable)** rather than as a win.
What remains open on GLM, in priority order, is what the pre-registration explicitly
could not resolve:

1. **A cool window, or a paired-difference design** that survives 30 pp of spread —
   the shared-expert barrier and any spinning-that-reads-as-busy remain the only
   unquantified per-token costs, and per the 2026-09-24 dispatch census the
   counters structurally cannot see them.
2. **The GLM prefill cell** as its own paired question (above).
3. Equal-count AB/BA with the first ~40 min of a session discarded — this run's
   rounds 4-6 were progressively more disturbed, so a second series is worth
   starting early in a machine's day rather than continuing late in this one.
