# fa-probe-1: what does llama-bench's `flash_attn:-1` (auto) actually resolve to?

Every campaign row so far has been compared against a llama arm invoked without
`-fa`, which reports `flash_attn: -1` in its own JSON. Auto is not documented
for the Metal backend, and the answer decides whether the llama numbers this
goal chases are llama's best attention path or a handicapped one. Probed by
running both settings, not by reading docs.

    llama-bench -m models/qwen3-0.6b-Q8_0.gguf -p PP -n 0 -d 0 -r 1 \
        -ngl 99 -ctk f16 -ctv f16 -fa {on,off} -o json

| PP   | -fa on | -fa off | on/off |
|------|--------|---------|--------|
| 8192 | 4966.5 | 2002.3  | 2.48x  |
| 256  | 6677.6 | 7095.3  | 0.94x  |

**Answer: auto = ON.** The campaign's llama arm at pp8192 reported 4928.7
(`scaling-1.sh` smoke, load 7.0); `-fa on` here gives 4966.5 and `-fa off`
gives 2002.3. The arm sits on the on-number, 2.5x above the off-number, so the
registered baseline has been flash attention all along.

Consequences:
- The llama prefill rows in `pp-sweep-1`, `pf-obuf-dense-1` and `series-1` are
  fair comparisons against llama's best attention path. No re-benchmarking is
  owed, and "closing the gap" is not closing an artifact of a disabled kernel.
- allpaka's fused prefill also runs a flash-style kernel (`attend_mm`,
  `ALLPAKA_ATTN_MM` default ON), so the long-context comparison is kernel vs
  kernel, not kernel vs materialised scores.
- At pp256 the two settings are indistinguishable (0.94x) and both rows are
  contention-damaged - load 21 during these runs, against 7.0 for the 10161
  smoke row. Only the pp8192 contrast is used above, because a 2.48x effect is
  far outside that noise. The pp256 pair is reported for completeness, not as
  a short-context result.

Caveat on method: one repeat per cell, taken during a contended window, chosen
because the expected effect (2x) is two orders above the window's noise. It is
not a certified measurement of llama's FA cost, and none is needed - the
question was which arm auto selects, and 4928 vs 2002 answers it unambiguously.
