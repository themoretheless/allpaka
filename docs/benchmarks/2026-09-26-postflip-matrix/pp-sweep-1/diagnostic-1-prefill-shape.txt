## The 0.41x was an instrument artifact, on three separate counts

1. Single sample. The rehearsal's 2467.93 tok/s was one sample at load 14.36.
   Re-measuring the same shape three times here gives 2309 / 3969 / 4017 -
   the rehearsal caught the low end of its own distribution. Median 3969, not
   2468.
2. PP=64 is a fixed-cost shape. The log partitions 28 ms of wall into 12 ms of
   CPU encode and 14 ms waited, for 281 dispatches over 28 layers. llama does
   the whole 64-token prefill in 10.5 ms, so allpaka's CPU encode ALONE is
   more than llama's entire answer at this shape.
3. The ratio moves with PP, which a kernel gap would not do:

    PP      allpaka med   llama med   ratio   allpaka dispatches/waits
    64          3969        6127      0.64x  281 / 29   (1 chunk)
    256         7734       10255      0.75x  281 / 29   (1 chunk)
    1024        8782        8972      0.97x  562 / 58   (2 chunks of 512)
    4096        5468        7326      0.74x  2248 / 232 (8 chunks of 512)

   At PP=1024 the GPU clock says executing 101 ms inside a 125 ms wall, while
   llama's whole 1024-token prefill takes 114 ms. allpaka's GPU is ahead of
   llama's end-to-end there; the wall is what loses, and it loses by 3%.

`docs/moe-prefill.md` already documents allpaka prefill at or above llama
parity on MoE (qwen35moe 1412 vs 1305 = 108%; qwen3-30b +6.8%; GLM at
parity), and it records 0.6B Q8_0 prefill at 9179 tok/s. 2468 was never a
throughput claim; it was 64 tokens of setup.

## What IS left over: a long-context prefill regression at PP=4096

This one is not fixed cost and does not go away with a bigger PP.

    PP=1024 -> PP=4096 is 4x the tokens.
      allpaka wall  125 -> 749 ms   = 6.0x
      llama   wall  114 -> 559 ms   = 4.9x

Attention is quadratic so both must exceed 4x, but allpaka exceeds it by
22% more than llama does. The GPU accounts for it, not the CPU: executing
725 ms of a 786 ms wall (92%), whereas at PP=1024 executing was 101 of
125 ms (81%). Consistent with a longer per-chunk fixed cost too - allpaka
runs n_batch 512 (8 chunks) where llama-bench runs 2048 (2 chunks).

Not certified: the same window at load <5 must re-run PP=1024 and PP=4096
before this is quoted as a gap, and the two arms' spreads at 4096 are wide
(allpaka 5213-6320, llama 6376-7559).

## Instrument defect found on the way: fused-prefill phase attribution

The `prefill phases` breakdown reports `ffn (experts)` at 86-91% at every PP
from 64 to 4096, and `attention` falling from 1.5% to 0.5%. Attention's FLOPs
grow ~400x across that sweep, so those percentages cannot be a GPU
attribution of the fused chunk - they are CPU-side spans, and in the fused
path the work is inside one command buffer. Do not use the phase table to
attribute fused-prefill time between FFN and attention; `ALLPAKA_FFN_TIME` /
`ALLPAKA_PF_SPLIT` (which serialise, and so inflate) are the only per-stage
timings that mean anything there, per moe-prefill.md's own caveat.
